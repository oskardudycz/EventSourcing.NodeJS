### Step-1. A rolled-back inner transaction leaves its version tracking behind
Quote: `Transaction.transaction`: `var changesBeforeInnerTransaction = new LinkedHashMap<>(changes); ... catch (Exception e) { changes.clear(); changes.putAll(changesBeforeInnerTransaction); throw e; }`; `change()` does `versionsSeen.computeIfAbsent(key, _ -> database.latestVersionOf(typeClass, id))`. Spec 20.4: "D is not stored and the outer transaction's own writes are". Q67 2: "none of its writes are recorded". Neither says what happens to the inner transaction's reads and version tracking.
Consequence: only `changes` is restored. `versionsSeen` and `typeChangesSeen` keep the inner entries. Outer stores D1, inner stores D2 and throws, outer catches, another thread stores D2, outer commits: `commit()` sees D2 changed and throws `ConcurrencyException`, although the outer transaction writes only D1. No test covers it.
Belongs to: spec (search of spec.md and qa.md found no decision on reads or version tracking after a caught inner failure)
Resolution: superseded: nested transactions are not supported (Oskar, "yes." to Q70 follow-up; step 16).
