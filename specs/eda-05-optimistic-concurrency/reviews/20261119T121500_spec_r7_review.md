### Quality-1. The guest stay facade can't see what `initiateGroupCheckout` stores, so AC 2.15 can't be tested without a new seam

Quote: AC 2.15: "THEN A and B are each stored, checked out, under their own ids, and nothing is stored under the group checkout id". Testing Decisions: "No new seam."
Consequence: A `Collection` keeps the inner map it got from `computeIfAbsent` when built; `transaction` copies every inner map and on commit replaces `this.storage` with the copies. `GuestStayAccountFacade` builds its collection once, so after a group checkout it still reads the old map: A and B look checked-in whether or not the fix is there. A developer must read the `Database` directly (a new seam), change how collections see committed transactions (undecided), or write a test that passes either way. Same for "nothing is stored" in AC 2.13.
Belongs to: qa
Resolution: fixed in r8: Oskar, "Why copying? What if two transactions happen in parallel? We need to simulate transactions, not full acid but good enough"; the transaction records changes and applies them on commit under a lock; AC 2.15 checks the fix through the facades.
