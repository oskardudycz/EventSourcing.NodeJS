### Q&A-1. Nothing says whether the rule applies to the JVM 03 Kafka, RabbitMQ and transactions solutions

Quote: AC 2.13: "GIVEN any 03–06 guest stay facade, any repository and flavour …". Solution: "check out in 03 to 06 comes from the bus and does nothing for a missing account, as 03 and 04 already do". Out of Scope: "The Kafka and RabbitMQ work in JVM, which goes last." 04 spec: "The Kafka and RabbitMQ solutions in JVM 03 … Acceptance criteria 1–3 don't apply to them."
Consequence: JVM `sagas_kafka` and `sagas_rabbitmq` guest stay facades are wired to the bus and throw "Entity not found" from `checkOutGuest`, and their group checkout facades throw for record completion and failure. The `transactions` checkout throws too, but nothing on the bus calls it (its group checkout facade calls it inside a transaction). A developer must guess whether to change them and add the 2.14 tests, and for `transactions` which branch of the rule applies.
Belongs to: qa
Resolution: fixed in r6: Oskar, "No don't leave them out, it has to be aligned"; the JVM 03 Kafka and RabbitMQ solutions follow the rule with its tests; transactions checkout is a direct call and keeps throwing.
