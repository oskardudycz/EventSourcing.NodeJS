### Q&A-1. "The storage lock" has no counterpart in the 03, 04 and 05 `Database`

Quote: "the commit takes the storage lock, checks every recorded expected version (05 only …)". Implementation Decision: "commit under the storage lock". Q53: "applies them together under the storage lock".
Consequence: 06 has one storage map, and every `get` and write goes through `commit` under `synchronized (storage)`. 03 and 04 take no lock (`Collection` works on the per-class map); 05 locks each per-class map. If the 05 commit locks something else, a plain `store(id, obj, expectedVersion)` can land between the commit's check and write and be overwritten without a `ConcurrencyException`, the lost update 05 teaches about. AC 2.16 doesn't catch it.
Belongs to: spec
Resolution: fixed in r10: Oskar, "cut the bs we're not teaching them databases transaction should just work" and "I told you 06 is out of scope numerous times"; the spec states what the transaction must do, not how, and doesn't point at 06.

### Q&A-2. Do 03, 04 and 05 gain the 06 `transaction` overload that returns a result?

Quote: "works as the JVM 06 `Database.transaction` already does". AC 2.2: "every operation of the 04 tools is still there (Q45, Q46), and the only differences are …".
Consequence: 06 has `<Result> Result transaction(Function<Database, Result>)` and `void transaction(Consumer<Database>)`; 03 and 04 only `Consumer`. Copying both adds an operation AC 2.2 doesn't name; keeping one is the other reading.
Belongs to: spec
Resolution: fixed in r10: Oskar, "cut the bs we're not teaching them databases transaction should just work" and "I told you 06 is out of scope numerous times"; the spec states what the transaction must do, not how, and doesn't point at 06.

### Quality-1. "The storage lock" doesn't exist in 03–05, so the developer has to choose how to lock

Quote: as Q&A-1; AC 2.16 (05): "another store of D lands before it commits THEN the commit throws `ConcurrencyException`".
Consequence: Options: (a) one flat map with one lock, as 06, which changes every direct `get`/`store`/`delete`; (b) per-class maps, commit locks every inner map it touches; (c) a database-level lock only the commit takes, which lets a direct 05 store slip between check and write. For 03/04 a lock on direct operations is extra work on every existing operation; Q52 allows a lock only for the commit.
Belongs to: qa
Resolution: fixed in r10: Oskar, "cut the bs we're not teaching them databases transaction should just work" and "I told you 06 is out of scope numerous times"; the spec states what the transaction must do, not how, and doesn't point at 06.

### Quality-2. "Fitted to each `Database`" doesn't say which parts of 06 to take

Quote: "works as the JVM 06 `Database.transaction` already does, fitted to each `Database` (Q52, Q53)"; "no copy of the storage".
Consequence: 06 also has the `Function` overload, a `Write` record with `key`/`type`/`position`, `TransientFailures.failIfUnavailable()` before the commit, and no try/catch. The spec doesn't say whether 03–05 get the overload, or that the 03/04 `Collection` stops holding the inner map and goes through the `Database` (needed to record writes). Two developers can build different signatures.
Belongs to: spec
Resolution: fixed in r10: Oskar, "cut the bs we're not teaching them databases transaction should just work" and "I told you 06 is out of scope numerous times"; the spec states what the transaction must do, not how, and doesn't point at 06.

### Quality-3. The named `Database` tests don't cover everything AC 2.16 requires

Quote: the five named tests; AC 2.16: "WHEN a transaction deletes D and commits THEN the collection doesn't return D"; "a successful transactional store of D leaves it at version 2"; "the exception reaches the caller and nothing is printed".
Consequence: No named test for a transactional delete or the version after a transactional store; "leaves the storage unchanged" doesn't say it checks the exception and the missing print. A commit that ignores deletes or doesn't bump the version passes.
Belongs to: spec
Resolution: fixed in r10: Oskar, "cut the bs we're not teaching them databases transaction should just work" and "I told you 06 is out of scope numerous times"; the spec states what the transaction must do, not how, and doesn't point at 06.
