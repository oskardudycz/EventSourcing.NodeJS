## Overview

The products: reserving the stock, asking the fulfillment service to send the package, and releasing the stock when the order is canceled. `FulfillmentService` keeps the warehouse's model out of this context. See the [context map](/docs/domains/e-commerce/1.0.0#context-map) for how it works with the others.
