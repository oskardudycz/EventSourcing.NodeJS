## Overview

The customer's cart: the products they picked, at the price each had when they added it. Checking out publishes `ShoppingCartCheckedOut`, the published language that the other contexts read. See the [context map](/docs/domains/e-commerce/1.0.0#context-map) for how it works with the others.
