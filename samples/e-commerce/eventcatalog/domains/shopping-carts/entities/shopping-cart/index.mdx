## Overview

The aggregate behind the shopping-carts module. [ShoppingCart](https://github.com/oskardudycz/EventSourcing.NodeJS/blob/main/samples/e-commerce/packages/application/src/shoppingCarts/shoppingCart.ts) is the state, folded from the cart's stream, and its decider (`decide` and `evolve` in the same file) decides the events.

## Prices

The customer chooses a product and a quantity, never a price. The price list gives the price when the product first enters the cart. Checking out reads the current prices: if one changed, the cart records `ProductItemPriceChanged` and stays open, and the customer checks out again with the new version. The [README](https://github.com/oskardudycz/EventSourcing.NodeJS/tree/main/samples/e-commerce#where-the-steps-come-from) explains why.
